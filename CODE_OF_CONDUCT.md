# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, trolling, personal or political
attacks, publishing others' private information, and any conduct that would
reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the
maintainers via a private GitHub report on this repository. All complaints
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers may
remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues and contributions
that violate this Code of Conduct.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
